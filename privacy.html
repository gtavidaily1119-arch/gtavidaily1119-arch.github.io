This Privacy Policy describes how the GTAVI Daily Uploader ("the App"), operated by the owner of the GTA VI Daily YouTube channel, handles information when it accesses YouTube through the YouTube API Services.

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the GTA VI Daily channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to, and does not collect data from, the general public.

2. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, the account holder agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the App accesses

  • An OAuth access token for the channel owner's own Google account, with permission to upload and manage videos on the GTA VI Daily channel.
  • Basic channel and video information needed to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing times, and confirm that uploads succeeded.

The App does not access viewers' personal data, comments, subscriber lists, or any other user's account.

4. How the data is used

Data is used solely to upload and schedule the channel's own videos and Shorts and to manage their metadata. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

5. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and credentials are stored only on the channel owner's own computer or private automation environment, in files that are not shared or published. No data is stored on third-party servers by the App.

6. Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Locally stored tokens are deleted when the App is no longer used, and in any case within 30 days of access being revoked.

8. Data deletion

The channel owner can delete all data stored by the App at any time by deleting the local token and database files, and by revoking access on the Google security settings page. Questions about data handling or deletion requests: gtavidaily1119@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.

9. Cookies

This website and the App do not use cookies or tracking technologies.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
